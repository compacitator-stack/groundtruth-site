What a Prop Firm Eval Pays If You Have No Edge

D18 · Test locked 2026-10-10 · Dated rules, conditional results

1. Question, scope and conditional registered answer

With no edge, through the first payout, on BluSky Launch 50K, rules retrieved 2026-10-10, the QQQ stand-in returned 58.2–64.2 cents per fee dollar at one trade a day. The simple model returned 59.8 cents. These are ratios of mean payouts to mean fees over purchases, including failures.

The price includes $59 per 30 days during evaluation, $99 on a pass, no paid reset, and 90% of a first payout of at least $500. Later payouts are a separate report-only result below. No edge means random entries, not a judgement about a trader.

We did not test JJ Simon's entries, skill or payouts. QQQ stands in for NQ futures; it is an assumption, not a verified futures replication.

2. JJ Simon's frame, source credits and what we grant

The claim comes from JJ Simon on Chart Fanatics, published 2026-10-04. He reports a 33% pass rate and a 91% chance of no payout; the complement is 9%. Those are his figures, not verified personal performance.

We grant his frame: a pass is a different objective from trading well; multiply pass probability by the chance of payment after passing; value a funded account over its paying life. His statement about value after payment ends here: “Because you still have expected value in your account after taking a payout. So net, you’re making a bunch of money”.

The source video’s title card says “$1.8 MILLION”. That is an attributed claim; this test does not audit the earnings. He names BluSky for his funded accounts, not the Launch plan for his described evaluation.
